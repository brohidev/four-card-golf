    How to play

    Keep the total value of your four cards as low as possible.

    1. At the start of each round, look once at your bottom two cards. Memorize them and hide them again. Play begins when everyone is ready.
    2. On your turn, draw from the stock or take the top discard. The drawn card is revealed to everyone.
    3. A stock card can replace any card in your grid, face up. The replaced card goes to the discard pile. Or discard the drawn card and flip one of your hidden cards instead.
    4. A card taken from the discard must replace a card in your grid, face up. You may replace a hidden or revealed card.
    5. When someone finishes a turn with all four cards face up, everyone else gets one final turn. Then all hands are revealed and scored.

    Card values: A = −1; 2–10 = their number; J and Q = 10; K = 0.

    Column pairs: Two cards with the same rank in the same vertical column score zero together. Suits do not matter. Horizontal and diagonal pairs do not cancel.

    Winning: The lowest score wins each round. Scores add up across rounds; the lowest total after the chosen number of rounds wins. Ties share the win.

    If the stock runs out, the discards are shuffled into a new stock, keeping the top discard. The starting player rotates each round.

    Refresh in the same browser to reconnect. A disconnected player keeps their seat and turn while they reconnect. Rooms are cleared when the server restarts.
